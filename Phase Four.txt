# Phase 4 — Manager Web Dashboard & Supervisor Portal

Telecalling CRM · Technical implementation specification · Web & Desktop-first

Phase 4 builds the business command center on top of the Phase 1 native Kotlin Telephony Engine, Phase 2 NestJS backend core, and Phase 3 telecaller mobile app. While Phase 1 proved that native Android telephony, Room outbox, and recording capture are rock-solid, Phase 2 established PostgreSQL, device-bound authentication, and offline synchronization, and Phase 3 delivered the telecaller mobile experience with local SQLite read models and two-tap dispositions, Phase 4 delivers the operational nerve center for managers, team supervisors, and administrators.

This phase transforms raw server telemetry, lead statuses, and S3 audio objects into a fast, intuitive, real-time web portal (Next.js 16) that empowers managers to supervise active shifts, audit recording quality, rebalance lead loads, and make strategic sales decisions without ever relying on disconnected spreadsheets.

Your master plan allocates approximately 2 to 3 weeks to this phase.

---

## Phase 4 exit criteria

# 100%
Real-time shift visibility across active telecallers with live metrics refreshed every 5–10s

# <2s
S3 audio playback latency for connected calls ≥10s via secure presigned streaming URLs

# 0
Lost leads during bulk CSV import, E.164 deduplication, and bulk round-robin reassignment

# ≥95%
Recording coverage audit visibility with clear automated distinction between MISSING and UNSUPPORTED_DEVICE

# 100%
Responsive console usability across modern desktop browsers and mobile Safari/Chrome (iPhone & iPad managers)

These criteria guarantee that sales managers and team leads have total operational control over telecalling shifts, call quality, lead progression, and hardware fleet health in real time.

## 1. What are we building in Phase 4?

We are building the complete Manager Web Dashboard in `apps/web` using **Next.js 16 (App Router)**, **React 19**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Lucide Plane Outline Icons**, interfacing directly with the NestJS API (`@telecalling-crm/api-client`) and MinIO/S3 object storage.

The manager application encompasses seven major functional pillars:

- **1. Live Shift Board & Real-Time Performance Monitor:**
  - Executive KPI summary cards: Total Leads, Calls Today, Connected Calls Today, Connect Rate %, Average Talk Duration, Pending Follow-ups, and Active Callers.
  - Live shift calling feed displaying recent dials, outcomes, and durations with real-time updates via smart polling (5–10s interval).
  - Active telecaller shift table: live status (Calling, Idle, Break, Stale), dials, connects, and current talk time.

- **2. Lead Command Center & Assignment Engine:**
  - Comprehensive searchable and paginated leads table.
  - Multi-attribute filtering by Pipeline Stage (`NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `INTERESTED`, `PROPOSAL_SENT`, `MEETING_SCHEDULED`, `WON`, `LOST`), Assignee, Date Range, and Search text.
  - Bulk actions: Select multiple leads and reassign them manually to a specific telecaller or distribute them evenly via weighted round-robin.
  - Bulk CSV/Excel lead import modal with column mapping, automated E.164 phone normalization, duplicate detection, and import error logs.

- **3. Lead Profile & Chronological Activity Timeline:**
  - Dedicated lead detail drawer / page showcasing full contact context, custom fields, estimated solar load (kW), and current pipeline stage.
  - Chronological activity timeline unifying every event in the lead lifecycle: call logs, telecaller remarks, two-tap dispositions, follow-up history, WhatsApp message status, and stage transition audit.
  - One-click access to listen to call recordings directly within the timeline.

- **4. Secure In-Browser Call Recording Audio Player:**
  - Embedded HTML5 audio player modal with waveform visualization and playback rate controls (`0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2.0x`).
  - Calls `GET /recordings/:id/url` to fetch short-lived (10-minute) presigned S3/MinIO GET URLs, ensuring zero direct public access to sensitive customer voice recordings.
  - Seamless support for `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.amr`, and `.wav` audio containers captured by OEM stock dialers.
  - Audio download prevention toggle enforced for standard telecallers and managers.

- **5. Team Telecaller & Hardware Fleet Health Audit:**
  - Telecaller performance scorecard: Dials today, connects, connect rate %, total talk time (formatted as `HH:mm:ss`), recorded calls, and recording coverage %.
  - Hardware device telemetry table: Model (e.g. Realme 12x 5G, Samsung Galaxy M34), Android version, App version, Engine version, Last seen watermark, and Clock offset drift.
  - Telemetry health indicators: Battery level, Unrestricted battery optimization status, SAF recording folder permission grant status, and local Room outbox backlog count.
  - Strict compliance badge separation: Green (`MATCHED` / `UPLOADED`), Red (`MISSING` on supported devices ≥10s), Gray (`NOT_APPLICABLE` for <10s or unanswered), and Amber (`UNSUPPORTED_DEVICE` for Google Dialer without file access).

- **6. Team Follow-Up Calendar & Task Dispatch:**
  - Overdue, Today, and Upcoming callback views for the entire sales floor.
  - Filter follow-ups by assigned telecaller or pipeline stage.
  - Reassign pending callbacks when telecallers are on leave or absent.
  - One-click callback completion and rescheduling from the web UI.

- **7. CRM Configuration & Administration:**
  - Manage configurable pipeline stages and business disposition types.
  - Enforce calling-hours window (9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST) and DPDP/TRAI consent rules.
  - User and device administration: Invite telecallers, assign roles (`admin`, `manager`, `telecaller`), and remotely revoke compromised devices.

### Task 1 — Manager Authentication, Session Management & RBAC Protection
**Timeline:** Days 1–3  
**Goal:** Establish secure web authentication with role-based access control, ensuring telecallers cannot view manager dashboards or company-wide audio.

- **Subtasks:**
  1. Build modern login screen in `apps/web/src/app/login/page.tsx` with email/password input, remember me, and instant error feedback.
  2. Implement JWT token persistence in `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` with automatic header injection via authenticated API wrapper.
  3. Enforce client-side route guards: redirect unauthenticated users to `/login`; restrict `/callers`, `/settings`, and S3 audio playback to `manager` and `admin` roles.
  4. Integrate automatic token expiration detection: if an API call returns `401 Unauthorized`, trigger silent refresh or redirect gracefully without corrupting form state.
  5. Add user profile header dropdown with role pill (`ADMIN`, `MANAGER`), user metadata, and safe sign-out.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Manager credentials successfully authenticate and persist across page refreshes.
  - Telecaller tokens attempting to load manager audit endpoints are rejected with `403 Forbidden`.
  - Sign-out clears tokens cleanly and returns to login screen.

### Task 2 — Live Shift Board & Smart Polling Architecture
**Timeline:** Days 3–6  
**Goal:** Deliver real-time shift visibility updated every 5–10 seconds without high server overhead or memory leaks.

- **Subtasks:**
  1. Build executive KPI metric cards in `StatCard.tsx`:
     - **Total Leads:** Global count in CRM.
     - **Calls Today:** Total calls dialed since 00:00:00 IST.
     - **Connected Calls Today:** Calls with outcome `CONNECTED` + connect rate percentage.
     - **Average Talk Duration:** Average seconds per connected call.
     - **Pending Follow-ups:** Uncompleted callbacks across all callers.
     - **Active Callers:** Number of telecallers with heartbeat seen in the last 30 minutes.
  2. Implement `useLiveBoard.ts` hook with smart polling:
     - Configurable 5–10s polling interval.
     - Automatic pause when manager browser tab is backgrounded / hidden (`document.hidden`).
     - Exponential backoff on network drop or server error.
     - Manual "Sync Now" button with spin animation.
  3. Build `ShiftCallingFeed.tsx`: Live chronological stream of the latest 20 calls showing caller name, lead name, masked phone number, outcome badge, talk duration, and timestamp.
  4. Build active telecaller shift grid showing caller avatars, active status (Calling, Idle, Break), calls made today, and last action.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Live board updates metrics every 5–10s without page reload.
  - Tab backgrounding pauses polling cleanly to conserve network and server CPU.
  - Ingesting new calls immediately increments "Calls Today" and "Connected Calls" on the next poll.

### Task 5 — Secure In-Browser Call Recording Audio Playback (S3 Presigned)
**Timeline:** Days 10–14  
**Goal:** Allow managers to listen to recorded calls securely in the browser within <2 seconds, with zero direct public access to S3.

- **Subtasks:**
  1. Connect `AudioPlayerModal.tsx` to `GET /recordings/:id/url` to retrieve short-lived (600s / 10-minute) presigned S3/MinIO streaming URLs.
  2. Implement HTML5 audio streaming controller with:
     - Play / Pause / Seek scrubber.
     - Current time / Total duration display.
     - Playback speed multiplier toggles: `0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2.0x`.
     - Volume control with mute toggle.
     - Waveform visualization indicator.
  3. Support audio codecs produced by Android OEM stock recorders:
     - Samsung: `.m4a` (AAC)
     - Xiaomi: `.mp3` / `.aac`
     - Vivo: `.amr` / `.wav`
     - Oppo/Realme: `.m4a` / `.mp3`
  4. Audio Security & Privacy Rules:
     - Audio player accessible only to users with role `admin` or `manager`.
     - Direct raw S3 bucket URLs are never exposed in DOM or HTML source.
     - Presigned URLs expire in 10 minutes, preventing leaked links from persisting.
     - `controlsList="nodownload"` enforced on audio element.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Clicking "Play Recording" on any connected call ≥10s opens the player and starts playback in <2 seconds.
  - Speed toggles (1.5x, 2.0x) adjust audio rate smoothly without pitch distortion.
  - Expired URLs automatically refresh transparently on replay.

### Task 6 — Hardware Fleet Telemetry & Recording Coverage Audit Table
**Timeline:** Days 13–16  
**Goal:** Track telecaller mobile phone health, battery levels, background sync lag, and audit recording coverage compliance per telecaller.

- **Subtasks:**

     - Detected Device Model (e.g. `Realme RMX3869`, `Samsung SM-M346B`).
     - App & Engine Version (e.g. `v1.0.0 (Engine 1.0)`).
     - Dials Today, Connects Today, and Connect Rate %.
     - Total Talk Time (e.g. `01h 42m 15s`).
     - Recorded Calls Count.
     - **Recording Coverage Percentage:**
       $$\text{Coverage} = \frac{\text{Recorded Calls}}{\text{Supported Connected Calls } \ge 10\text{s}} \times 100\%$$
  2. Build compliance badge logic:
     - **Green Badge (≥95%):** High recording health.
     - **Red Badge (<95%):** Recording deficit on supported phone; highlights missing audio files for manager investigation.
     - **Amber Badge (`UNSUPPORTED_DEVICE`):** Telecaller using Google Dialer without SAF file access. Clearly informs manager that calls log accurately, but audio sync is unsupported by hardware dialer.
  3. Build `DeviceHealthCard.tsx` consuming `GET /devices`:
     - Battery percentage & charging state.
     - Battery optimization state (Warning if not Unrestricted).
     - Storage Access Framework (SAF) folder grant status.
     - Clock offset drift (`clockOffsetMs`).
     - Local Room outbox pending mutation count (flags devices failing to sync).
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Telecallers on Google Dialer are clearly marked with an amber `UNSUPPORTED_DEVICE` badge and not penalized on recording coverage.
  - Devices with >10 pending outbox mutations or stale heartbeat (>2 hours) display prominent warning indicators.

## 7. UX / UI Design Specifications & Modern Light Guidelines

To maintain complete visual harmony with the mobile caller app developed in Phase 3, the manager web dashboard follows the exact same **Modern Light Design System**:

- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: Crisp, clean `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) and `#FFFFFF`.
  - Borders: Subtle `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) dividers providing structure without harsh contrast.
  - Primary Accent: Royal Indigo / Blue `#4F46E5` / `#4338CA` for active tabs, primary action buttons, and active selection states.
  - Success: Mint Emerald `#10B981` (Connected calls, Won deals, Sync Healthy).
  - Warning: Warm Amber `#F59E0B` (Pending sync, Reassignment needed, `UNSUPPORTED_DEVICE`).
  - Danger: Soft Coral Rose `#EF4444` (Lost leads, Overdue follow-ups, `MISSING` recording alert).
  - Neutral Text: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for primary headings; `#64748B` (Slate 500) for secondary metadata.

- **Typography & Layout:**
  - Clean sans-serif system font stack (`Inter`, `system-ui`).
  - Card elevation: Soft drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`).
  - High information density without visual clutter: compact table rows (`py-3`), rounded pill badges, and clear column alignments.

- **Icons:**
  - **Lucide Plane Outline Icons** (`stroke-width={1.75}`) used exclusively across all navigation tabs, action buttons, table columns, and audio controls.

- **Audio Player UX:**
  - Elegant sticky bottom player or centered modal overlay.
  - Displays prospect name, phone, caller name, call timestamp, and duration.
  - Single-click speed toggles (`1x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2x`).
  - Scrub bar with current time and remaining time counters.

## 9. Security, Privacy & Failure Modes

- **1. Presigned S3 URL Expiration & Security:**
  - Raw S3 buckets remain completely private with zero public read access.
  - Playback URLs expire automatically in 10 minutes (`600s`).
  - `GET /recordings/:id/url` is restricted strictly to users with role `admin` or `manager`. Telecallers cannot request arbitrary audio URLs.
  - Web client applies `controlsList="nodownload"` on the `<audio>` element to discourage unapproved local saving of customer voice calls.

- **2. High-Frequency Polling Database Safety:**
  - Smart polling (5–10s) targets indexed summary endpoints (`GET /reports/summary`, `GET /reports/callers`).
  - Client pauses polling immediately when the browser tab is hidden or minimized (`document.hidden === true`).
  - Queries leverage PostgreSQL indexed columns (`startedAt`, `outcome`, `role`, `active`) ensuring query execution latency <15ms under high concurrency.

- **3. CSV Import Protection & Memory Safety:**
  - File size restricted to 10MB per upload (~50,000 lead rows).
  - Streamed or chunked batch ingestion (batches of 100) prevents Node.js heap exhaustion.
  - Phone numbers validated and normalized to E.164. Invalid rows are isolated into an error report without aborting the valid remainder of the batch.

- **4. DPDP & Regulatory Audio Audit Logging:**
  - Every presigned audio URL generation is logged with timestamp, manager ID, and target `recordingId` to ensure a permanent audit trail of who listened to which customer conversation.
  - Recording disclosure status verified: calls without confirmed verbal disclosure display a warning icon on the manager review timeline.
